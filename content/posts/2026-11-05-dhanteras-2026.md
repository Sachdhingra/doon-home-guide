Dhanteras Diwali ka pehla din hai, aur zyada-tar gharon mein is din kuch naya laane ki parampara hai: sona-chaandi, bartan, jhaadu, ya ghar ke liye koi badi cheez. Dehradun mein bhi Paltan Bazaar se lekar Rajpur Road tak is din sabse zyada bheed hoti hai.

Is guide mein Dhanteras 2026 ki date aur muhurat ke saath ek practical sawal ka jawab hai: agar aap is Dhanteras ghar ke liye **tijori (home locker) ya steel almirah** lena chahte hain, to kya dekhein.

## Dhanteras par kya kharidna shubh maana jaata hai

- **Sona, chaandi, sikke:** sabse purani parampara.
- **Pital (brass) aur steel ke bartan:** zyada-tar parivaar inhe shubh maante hain.
- **Jhaadu:** Lakshmi ji ke swagat ka prateek maana jaata hai.
- **Dhaniya ke beej:** kai gharon mein puja ke baad gamle mein bote hain.
- **Ghar ki tijori ya locker:** dhan ko surakshit rakhne ki jagah, Lakshmi puja se seedha juda hua.
- **Electronics aur gaadi:** aajkal bahut log isi din booking karte hain.

Kuch parivaar is din lohe ki nukeeli cheezein avoid karte hain. Ye har ghar ki apni parampara hai.

## Tijori ya locker lene se pehle 5 checks

Tijori aisi cheez hai jo saalon tak ek hi jagah rehti hai, isliye jaldbaazi mein mat lijiye.

1. **Kya rakhna hai?** Sirf gehne aur cash ke liye chhota digital locker kaafi hai. Property ke kaagaz, passport aur gehne sab ek saath rakhne hain to bada locker ya locker wala steel almirah dekhiye.
2. **Lock ka type:** manual (chaabi), digital (PIN), ya biometric (fingerprint). Ghar mein bade-buzurg ho to PIN ke saath chaabi wala backup zaroori hai.
3. **Fix kaise hoga:** achha locker deewar ya farsh mein bolt hota hai. Kiraye ke ghar mein ho to bolt-down ke liye makaan maalik se poochh lijiye.
4. **Jagah:** almari ke andar, bed ke paas, ya puja ghar ke paas. Naap pehle le lijiye.
5. **Warranty aur service:** battery khatam ho jaaye ya PIN bhool jaayein to kaun aayega? Local dealer se lena isliye behtar hai.

## Locker vs locker wala steel almirah

| | Home locker / safe | Steel almirah (locker ke saath) |
|---|---|---|
| Kis ke liye | Gehne, cash, documents | Kapde + gehne + documents, sab ek jagah |
| Jagah | Kam (almari ke andar bhi fit) | 2-door ke liye lagbhag 3 feet chaudi deewar |
| Suraksha | Zyada (mazboot body, bolt-down) | Theek (andar ka locker, mazboot lock) |
| Budget (HDE par MRP from) | ₹4,790 se | ₹36,000 se |
| Dehradun ki nami mein | Steel, seelan ka asar nahi | Steel, deemak aur seelan dono se bachav |

Dehradun ke purane gharon mein jahan deewar mein seelan aati hai, wahan steel almirah lakdi ki almari se zyada tikti hai, kyunki usme na deemak lagti hai na nami se phoolti hai.

## Dhanteras par furniture kharidne ke smart tips

- **Booking ek din pehle:** Dhanteras ke din showroom mein sabse zyada bheed hoti hai. 4-5 November ko model final kar lijiye.
- **Delivery date likhwa lijiye:** agar usi din delivery na ho, to booking slip par date likhwa lijiye. Bahut parivaar shubh muhurat mein booking karke delivery baad mein lete hain.
- **Naap le kar jaaiye:** almirah ke liye deewar ki chaudai aur uchaai, darwaze aur seedhi ki chaudai bhi.
- **Exchange poochh lijiye:** purani almari ya sofa ke badle exchange offer chal raha ho to kaafi bachat hoti hai.
