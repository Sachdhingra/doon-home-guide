Diwali ke baad Dev Uthani Ekadashi aate hi shaadiyon ka season shuru ho jaata hai. Card, venue aur catering ke beech ek cheez aksar aakhri hafte tak reh jaati hai: **naye couple ke kamre ka furniture**. Aur tab tak ready stock khatam ho chuka hota hai ya delivery date shaadi ke baad ki milti hai.

Is guide mein pehle 2026-27 ki vivah muhurat dates, phir ek simple timeline ki furniture kab order karein.

## Vivah muhurat 2026-27: shubh dates

| Mahina | Shubh vivah dates |
|---|---|
| November 2026 | 21, 24, 25, 26 |
| December 2026 | 2, 3, 4, 11, 12 (kuch panchang mein 1, 5, 6, 13 bhi) |
| Mid-Dec 2026 se mid-Jan 2027 | Kharmas: shaadi ke muhurat nahi |
| January 2027 | 18, 19, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, 31 |
| February 2027 | 2, 10, 12, 15, 16, 20, 21, 27, 28 |

Ye dates alag-alag panchang sources ko milakar di gayi hain, aur sources mein 1-2 din ka fark ho sakta hai. Shaadi ki date aur samay hamesha apne pandit se kundli ke hisaab se confirm karein.

**Dhyaan dein:** December ki dates 12-13 tak hi hain. Uske baad lagbhag ek mahine ka gap hai, isliye December wali shaadiyon ka furniture November mein hi order ho jaana chahiye.

## Furniture order karne ki timeline

| Shaadi se kitne din pehle | Kya karein |
|---|---|
| 6-8 hafte | Kamre ka naap lijiye, budget tay kijiye, list banaiye |
| 5-6 hafte | Custom size wardrobe ya modular furniture order kijiye |
| 3-4 hafte | Bed, mattress, dressing table, sofa (ready stock) order kijiye |
| 2 hafte | Delivery date confirm kijiye, kamre ka paint aur safai poori kijiye |
| 7-10 din | Delivery aur fitting. Koi kami ho to theek karwane ka time bachta hai |
| Shaadi ka hafta | Sirf bedsheet, curtains aur sajavat |

Ready stock furniture aksar hafte bhar mein aa jaata hai, lekin season mein popular models jaldi khatam hote hain. Exact delivery date dukaan se likhwa kar lijiye.

## Naye couple ke kamre ki checklist

- **Bed:** 10x10 feet ke kamre mein queen size (60x78 inch), 12x12 ya bade mein king size (72x78 inch). Storage wala bed lijiye, Dehradun ke chhote flats mein bahut kaam aata hai.
- **Mattress:** bed ke size ka hi. Medium-firm aam taur par dono ke liye theek rehta hai.
- **Wardrobe:** do logon ke liye kam se kam 3-door. Jagah kam ho to sliding door.
- **Dressing table:** mirror ke saath, drawer wala.
- **Bedside tables:** do, phone charging ke liye switch ke paas.
- **Extra:** shoe rack, chhota sofa ya ottoman, aur ek locker gehnon ke liye.

## Budget ke hisaab se idea

| Budget | Kya aa sakta hai (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| ₹1 lakh tak | Storage bed + mattress combo, 2-door wardrobe |
| ₹1-2 lakh | Upar wala + dressing table + bedside tables, achha mattress |
| ₹2-3 lakh+ | Hydraulic/premium bed, 3-4 door wardrobe, dressing table, sitting set |

Ye range Godrej Interio ki MRP ke hisaab se hai. Offer aur combo par final price kam ho sakti hai.
