## Budget ke hisaab se kaunsi almirah

**₹15,000 se ₹30,000:** Storofix, StorEase, Neolite, Ultraline NX. Kiraye ke ghar, bachchon ke kamre, ya guest room ke liye theek. Shelves aur hanging rod milte hain, extra features kam.

**₹30,000 se ₹45,000:** yahi sabse zyada bikne wali range hai. Slimline 2-door (mirror ke saath ya bina), Storwel M2 aur Slide N Stack. Andar locker, achhi finish aur rang ke option.

**₹50,000 se upar:** 3-door Slimline, XL with locker, Auraline Enov (biometric) aur Centurion. Bade parivaar, shaadi ke baad naye kamre, ya jinhe gehne aur kaagzaat ke liye mazboot locker chahiye.

## 2 door vs 3 door: kaunsi lein

| | 2-door | 3-door |
|---|---|---|
| Chaudai | lagbhag 3 ft | lagbhag 4.5 ft |
| Kiske liye | 1-2 log | Couple + bachche, ya bahut kapde |
| Kamra | 10x10 ft ya chhota | 11x12 ft ya bada |
| Daam | ₹14,000 se ₹79,000 | ₹54,000 se ₹93,000 |

Ek seedha niyam: agar shaadi ke baad couple ke kapde ek hi almirah mein aane hain, to 3-door ya do 2-door lijiye. 2-door mein ek insaan ke kapde aaram se aate hain.

**4 door almirah?** Ghar ki range mein Godrej Interio 4-door steel almirah nahi banata. Do 2-door almirah ek line mein lagana sabse aam tareeka hai, aur ek kharab bhi ho to doosri chalti rehti hai.

## Size: almirah kitni jagah leti hai

Godrej Slimline 2-door ka size lagbhag **195 cm x 90 cm x 51 cm** (unchai x chaudai x gehrai) hai, yaani karib 6.4 ft unchi, 3 ft chaudi aur 1.7 ft gehri. Zyada-tar 2-door models isi ke aas-paas hain.

Khareedne se pehle ye naap lijiye:

1. **Deewar ki chaudai** jahan almirah lagegi.
2. **Chhat ya parchhatti ki unchai.** Purane Dehradun gharon mein parchhatti 6.5 ft par hoti hai, isliye unchai zaroor dekhiye.
3. **Saamne ki khuli jagah:** darwaza poora khulne ke liye kam se kam 2.5 ft. Jagah kam hai to sliding darwaze wali Slide N Stack lijiye.
4. **Seedhi aur darwaza:** almirah upar ki manzil par jaani hai to seedhi ka mod dekh lijiye.

## Dehradun ke liye steel almirah kyun

- **Nami aur seelan:** monsoon mein wooden furniture phool sakta hai. Steel nahi phoolta.
- **Deemak:** Doon mein deemak aam hai, steel par asar nahi hota.
- **Lambi umar:** powder coating wali almirah dashakon chalti hai, aur resale value bhi rehti hai.

> Dehradun tip: almirah ko deewar se 2-3 inch door rakhiye aur neeche plastic ke chhote pad lagaiye. Isse peeche hawa chalti hai aur farsh ki nami neeche ki coating tak nahi pahunchti.

## Khareedte waqt ye 5 cheezein check karein

1. **Locker:** andar ka locker alag chaabi se band hota hai ya nahi.
2. **Hanging rod aur shelves:** kitni shelves hain, aur kya unchai badal sakte hain.
3. **Darwaze ka alignment:** showroom mein khol-band karke dekhiye, awaaz ya ragad na ho.
4. **Coating:** kono par chips ya scratch to nahi.
5. **Warranty aur delivery:** delivery ke saath fitting aur level karna shaamil hai ya nahi.
