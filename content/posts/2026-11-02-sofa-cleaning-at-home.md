Diwali ki safai mein pankhe, khidkiyan aur rasoi to sab yaad rakhte hain, lekin **sofa** aksar aakhri din tak reh jaata hai. Aur yahi wo cheez hai jahan mehmaan sabse pehle baithte hain. Achhi baat ye hai ki zyada-tar sofa ghar ki cheezon se hi, 1-2 ghante mein, kaafi saaf ho jaate hain.

Ye guide Dehradun ke gharon ko dhyan mein rakh kar likhi hai, jahan monsoon ke baad sofa mein nami aur halki badboo aam baat hai.

## Shuru karne se pehle: 3 zaroori baatein

1. **Label dekhiye.** Sofa ke neeche ya cushion ki zip ke paas cleaning label hota hai. "Dry clean only" likha ho to paani kam se kam use karein.
2. **Patch test karein.** Koi bhi solution pehle peeche ya neeche ki chhoti jagah par lagakar 10 minute dekhiye. Rang nikle ya daag bane to wo solution mat lagaiye.
3. **Dhoop wala din chuniye.** November mein Dehradun mein din saaf aur sookhe hote hain. Khidkiyan khol kar safai karein taaki sofa jaldi sookhe. Leatherette ko seedhi dhoop mein mat rakhiye.

## Saaman jo chahiye

| Cheez | Kis kaam ki |
|---|---|
| Vacuum cleaner ya naram brush | Dhool aur baal nikalne ke liye |
| Baking soda | Badboo aur nami sokhne ke liye |
| Liquid dishwash ya mild shampoo | Halki safai ke liye |
| Safed sirka (vinegar) | Fabric par daag ke liye |
| 2-3 microfibre kapde | Ponchhne aur sukhaane ke liye |
| Spray bottle | Solution halka chhidakne ke liye |

## Fabric sofa saaf karne ka tareeka (step by step)

1. **Vacuum ya brush:** cushion hata kar kono, seams aur neeche ki jagah se dhool nikaliye. Yahi 60% safai hai.
2. **Baking soda:** poore sofa par patli layer chhidkiye, 30 minute se 2 ghante chhod dijiye, phir vacuum kar lijiye.
3. **Solution banaiye:** 1 mug gungune paani mein 1 chhota chammach liquid dishwash. Spray bottle mein bhar lijiye.
4. **Halka spray, bhigona nahi:** ek hisse par halka spray karke microfibre kapde se gol-gol nahi, ek hi direction mein ponchhiye.
5. **Saaf paani se ponchha:** doosre kapde ko saade paani mein bhigo kar, nichod kar, sabun ka asar hataiye.
6. **Sukhaiye:** pankha chala kar ya khidki khol kar 4-6 ghante. Sookhne se pehle baithiye mat.

> Dehradun tip: agar sofa monsoon ke baad se halka sa seela lagta hai, to safai ke baad 2-3 din din mein khidki khuli rakhiye. Deewar se 3-4 inch door rakhne se bhi nami kam aati hai.

## Leatherette (synthetic leather) sofa

- Naram kapde ko halke sabun-paani mein bhigo kar, achhe se nichod kar ponchhiye.
- Turant sookhe kapde se ponchh dijiye. Paani jama na hone dein.
- Mahine mein ek baar leatherette/leather conditioner lagane se crack nahi padte.
- **Kabhi nahi:** spirit, sanitiser, acetone, bleach. Ye coating ko sukha kar chhilka utaar dete hain.

## Velvet aur suede jaisa fabric

- Hamesha naram brush se, fabric ke "nap" (resho ki direction) ke saath brush karein.
- Paani bahut kam. Daag ho to sirf halka geela kapda aur turant sukhaiye.
- Steam iron ki halki steam (fabric chhue bina) se resha phir se khada ho jaata hai.

## Aam daag aur unka ilaaj

| Daag | Kya karein |
|---|---|
| Chai / coffee | Turant tissue se dabaiye (ragdiye mat). Phir 1:1 sirka-paani se halka ponchhiye. |
| Tel / ghee | Upar cornflour ya baking soda daal kar 20 minute chhodiye, phir brush karein. Baaki daag dishwash se. |
| Pen ki syahi | Fabric par thoda sa sanitiser cotton se dabaiye (pehle patch test). Leatherette par ye mat karein. |
| Bachchon ki crayon | Upar se butter paper rakh kar halki garam iron. Wax kaagaz mein chala jaata hai. |
| Pet ki smell | Baking soda raat bhar, phir vacuum. Zaroorat ho to enzyme cleaner. |

## Kab professional cleaning bulayein

- Sofa bahut bada hai (L-shape, 7-seater) aur poora gandha hai.
- Fabric "dry clean only" hai.
- Fungus ke dhabbe ya andar tak badboo hai.

Dehradun mein sofa shampooing ka kharcha aam taur par seat ke hisaab se lagta hai. 2-3 jagah se rate le lijiye.

## Kab safai nahi, naya sofa behtar hai

Safai rang aur smell theek kar sakti hai, lekin ye problems nahi:

- Baithne par cushion mein gaddha ban jaata hai aur wapas nahi uthta (foam bait gaya hai).
- Frame se chee-chee awaaz aati hai ya sofa hilta hai.
- Leatherette ka chhilka utar raha hai.
- Fabric phat gaya hai ya rang bahut ud gaya hai.

Aisa hai to purane sofa ko exchange mein dekar naya lena aksar sasta padta hai, kyunki naye foam aur frame ke saath aap 7-10 saal ke liye tension-free ho jaate hain.

## Ek line mein

Pehle vacuum, phir baking soda, phir halka sabun-paani: bhigona nahi, sirf ponchhna. Leatherette par kabhi spirit ya sanitiser nahi. Aur agar foam baith gaya hai, to safai nahi, badlaav ka time hai.
