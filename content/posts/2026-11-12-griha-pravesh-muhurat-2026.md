Dehradun mein pichhle kuch saalon mein Sahastradhara Road, GMS Road, Shimla Bypass aur Haridwar Road par naye flats aur villas tezi se bane hain. Aur bahut se parivaar Diwali ke baad, shaadi season ke saath, naye ghar mein **griha pravesh** karte hain.

Is guide mein teen cheezein hain: Nov-Dec 2026 ke shubh muhurat, shift karne se pehle furniture ki checklist, aur agar aap mehmaan hain to kya gift dein.

## Griha pravesh muhurat: November-December 2026

| Mahina | Shubh dates |
|---|---|
| November 2026 | 14, 20, 21, 25, 26 (11 Nov bhi tha) |
| December 2026 | 2, 3, 4, 11, 12, 18, 19, 30 |

Ye dates panchang sources se li gayi hain. Har date ka samay (muhurat ka window) alag hota hai, aur kai parivaar kharmas mein griha pravesh nahi karte. Apne pandit se kundli aur ghar ki disha ke hisaab se confirm zaroor karein.

## Griha pravesh ke din kya chahiye (furniture ke hisaab se)

Puja ke din ghar khaali bhi ho to chalega. Bas ye taiyaar rakhiye:

- Puja ki jagah ke liye ek chhoti chowki ya mandir.
- Mehmaanon ke baithne ke liye kuch chairs ya durrie.
- Rasoi mein doodh ubaalne ke liye gas ya induction aur ek bartan (parampara ke liye zaroori).

## Shift karne ke pehle hafte ka furniture checklist

| Kamra | Pehle hafte zaroori | Baad mein bhi le sakte hain |
|---|---|---|
| Bedroom | Bed + mattress, wardrobe | Dressing table, bedside tables |
| Living room | Sofa ya 4 chairs | Centre table, TV unit |
| Rasoi | Modular ya basic storage, gas | Crockery unit |
| Dining | Chhota 4-seater ya folding table | 6-seater dining set |
| Entrance | Shoe rack | Console, mirror |

**Tip:** pehle hafte ki list chhoti rakhiye. Ghar mein 2-3 hafte reh kar pata chalta hai ki dhoop kahan aati hai, seelan kahan hai, aur furniture kahan sabse achha lagega.

## Dehradun ke naye flats ke liye 4 practical baatein

1. **Lift aur darwaze naapiye:** bada sofa ya 3-door wardrobe lift mein jaayega ya nahi, ye pehle dekh lijiye.
2. **Seelan check:** naye flat mein bhi pehle monsoon mein deewar se nami aa sakti hai. Wardrobe ko deewar se 2-3 inch door rakhiye, aur nami wali deewar par lakdi ka furniture mat lagaiye.
3. **Dhoop wali jagah:** Doon mein sardi mein dhoop keemti hai. Sofa aur baithne ki jagah dhoop wali khidki ke paas rakhiye.
4. **Storage zyada socho:** pahadi gharon mein razai, kambal aur sweaters ki bahut jagah lagti hai. Storage wala bed aur upar loft wali wardrobe isi kaam aati hai.
