Furniture showroom mein sabse aam sawal: "Bhaiya, double bed ka size kya hai?" Aur aksar jawab galat ho jaata hai, kyunki India mein "double bed" teen alag size ke liye bola jaata hai. Naya bed lene se pehle ye ek page padh lijiye, galat mattress ya kamre mein na samane wala bed lene se bach jaayenge.

## Bed aur mattress size chart (India)

Mattress ki lambai India mein teen standard hoti hai: **72, 75 ya 78 inch**. Bed ka asli naam chaudai se tay hota hai.

| Size | Inch (L x W) | Feet | CM (78 inch lambai par) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | 72/75/78 x 36 | 6-6.5 x 3 ft | 198 x 91 |
| Double | 72/75/78 x 48 | 6-6.5 x 4 ft | 198 x 122 |
| Queen | 72/75/78 x 60 | 6-6.5 x 5 ft | 198 x 152 |
| King | 72/75/78 x 72 | 6-6.5 x 6 ft | 198 x 183 |

Sabse zyada chalne wala size: **queen 78 x 60** aur **king 78 x 72**. Bazaar mein zyada-tar beds isi hisaab se bante hain.

> Yaad rakhne ka asaan tareeka: 3 ft = single, 4 ft = double, 5 ft = queen, 6 ft = king.

## "Double bed" ka confusion

Bazaar mein "double bed" ka matlab hota hai "do log so sakein", na ki 48 inch ka size. Dukaan wala aksar queen ya king ko hi double bed bolta hai. Isliye:

- Bed ya mattress khareedte waqt **naam nahi, chaudai inch mein** poochhiye.
- Asli 48 inch double bed aajkal kam bikta hai. Do adult ke liye ye tight hai, ek adult + bachche ke liye theek.

## King vs queen: kaunsa lein

| | Queen | King |
|---|---|---|
| Chaudai | 60 inch (5 ft) | 72 inch (6 ft) |
| Lambai | 78 inch (6.5 ft) | 78 inch (6.5 ft) |
| Har insaan ke hisse | 30 inch | 36 inch |
| Kamra (kam se kam) | 10 x 10 ft | 12 x 12 ft |
| Kiske liye | Couple, chhota master bedroom | Couple + chhota bachcha, ya jinhe khula sona pasand hai |

Seedhi baat: agar bachcha aapke saath sota hai ya kisi ki neend halki hai, to king. Kamra 11 ft se chhota hai, to queen.

## Kamre ke size ke hisaab se bed

Bed ke do taraf aur paayon ki taraf kam se kam **2 ft chalne ki jagah** rakhiye. Almirah ho to uske saamne 2.5 ft aur.

| Kamre ka size | Sahi bed |
|---|---|
| 8 x 10 ft | Single ya double (4 ft) |
| 10 x 10 ft | Queen |
| 10 x 12 ft | Queen + almirah aaram se |
| 12 x 12 ft ya bada | King |

Dehradun ke kai naye flats mein master bedroom 11 x 12 ft ya usse bada hota hai, jahan king aa jaata hai, aur doosra bedroom 10 x 10 ke aas-paas, jahan queen behtar hai. Phir bhi apna kamra khud naap kar hi decide kijiye.

## Mattress naapne ka sahi tareeka

1. Purana mattress hata dijiye.
2. Bed ke andar ki khali jagah (plywood base) ko **andar ke kinare se kinare tak** naapiye.
3. Lambai aur chaudai **inch** mein likhiye.
4. Usi size ka mattress lijiye. 1 inch chhota chalega, bada kabhi nahi.

Purana mattress naap kar order karna sabse aam galti hai, kyunki saalon mein wo dab kar ya phail kar size badal leta hai.

## Mattress kitna mota ho

- **5-6 inch:** guest room, bachchon ka bed, ya diwan.
- **6-8 inch:** roz sone ke liye sabse balanced.
- **8 inch se zyada:** premium feel, par storage bed ki unchai badh jaati hai.
